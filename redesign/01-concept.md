# 01 — Concept & Art Direction

## One-liner
A first-person view from a rustic wooden porch onto a quiet, one-horse Texas Old West main street, drawn in the style of a 1940s Tex Avery *Droopy* Western background.

## Style rules
- **Line:** hand-inked dark outlines (ink ≈ `--ink`) and slightly wobbly, confident strokes.
- **Palette:** warm sepia, ochre, dusty desert and sun-bleached wood. See 03-tokens.md.
- **Texture:** subtle cel-painted watercolor grain, with no photographic texture.
- **Perspective:** exaggerated cartoon perspective, with the street receding to a central vanishing point.
- **Intensity:** a *soft vintage background painting*. The scene sits behind content, so it must never compete with the text (D2).

## Scene composition (back to front)
1. **Sky.** A warm, pale gold gradient with a few soft stylized clouds.
2. **Horizon.** Open desert mesas.
3. **Street.** A dusty main street receding to center, with weathered saloons and false-front buildings flanking left and right (one "OLD WEST" false front, saloon doors, water trough, hitching rail).
4. **Porch frame.** Wooden porch posts at the left and right edges, an overhang/eave across the top, floorboards across the bottom, and hitching rails at mid-height. This frame is what makes it feel like "you just stepped outside".
5. **Hanging sign.** A rough-hewn plank reading **Bronius**, hung by two thick ropes from the overhang at top center.
6. **Cloud nav.** Directly beneath the sign, a chain of cartoon clouds carrying the five nav links in crisp dark serif type.

## Props vocabulary
These props carry content. Every one is a container for **live text**, never a picture of text.

| Prop | Character | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Trail sign (arrow plank on post) | Directional, punchy | Section links (Music) |
| Plank sign (rectangular on post) | Solid, headline | Section links (Professional) |
| Parchment scroll (rolled ends) | Proclamation | Intro statement |
| Pinned parchment note (deckle edge, nail) | Personal, informal | Section links (Life & Faith) |
| Notice board (dark planks, nailed) | Container | Recent posts, subpage frame |
| Polaroid (pinned, slight tilt) | Snapshot | Random Flickr photo |
| Wanted poster (parchment, slab header, spot art) | Hero document | Subpage body |

Spot illustrations (e.g. violin and lantern) are hand-inked, sepia, and framed inside a poster.
