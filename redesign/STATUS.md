## Done: art pipeline ✅ (2026-11-14)
- **`_tools/art/build.py`** turns masters into `assets/img/art/` (80 files, AVIF + WebP at 1×/2×) plus **`_data/art.yml`**. It is deterministic (stable digest across runs) and takes about 45s. `--debug` dumps keyed PNGs to `/tmp/art-debug`. A subset build (`build.py sign-plank`) skips the yml.
- **Key:** greenness `g = G − max(R,B)` against a **local key-colour field** (a masked Gaussian of sure-bg pixels), so the polaroid gradient and interior holes work. Coverage is the max of the absolute (`g/g_K`) and chromatic (`(g/G)/(g_K/G_K)`) estimates. The chromatic one keys the shaded green in board-small's plank gaps and removes JPEG 4:2:0 green bleed on ink. Then un-mix `C=(I−(1−α)K)/α`, despill, choke 1px, feather σ0.5, and drop islands under 1% of the largest.
- **Clean:** the Gemini sparkle never showed up. The plates' **eave hooks are painted out** (feathered clone patch from the same rows), because their spacing would force a sign 0.6× (desktop) to 1.3× (mobile) the viewport. The ropes run up over the eave instead, as in the mockup.
- **Widths** come from the intended rendered box × 2 (comments in `ASSETS`). Budgets met: every prop is ≤ 40 KB AVIF @2×; home above-the-fold is ≈ 310 KB (budget 450); the whole home page is ≈ 350 KB (budget 700).
- **`art.yml`** gives `width`/`height` (largest size), `sizes[]` with avif/webp URLs, `box` (measured text/content box in %, for sign/trail planks and the polaroid window) and `slice` (`board-frame` 9-slice, `poster-sheet` vertical 3-slice; use `pct` in CSS).
- **Preview:** `redesign/reference/build_preview.py` generates `preview.html` from `art.yml`. Its `picture()` helper is the prototype for `_includes/picture.html`. The artifact was republished (`--prefix art/`, publish_files with only the referenced files).
