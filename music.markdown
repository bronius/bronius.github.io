Music has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I studied
classical violin from childhood, have played with many local ensembles, and have
always sung with the strongest chorales available to me.

Today that music life includes performing as Principal Second Violin with the
[Brazos Civic Orchestra](https://brazoscivicorchestra.org/), staying connected
to the broader local arts community through organizations like the
[Arts Council of Brazos Valley](https://www.acbv.org/), and teaching through my
[Brazos Valley Violin Studio](/violin-studio.html).

The studio exists because students often need more than information. They may be
carrying confidence gaps, knowledge gaps, or simply the fear that everyone else
already knows how this is supposed to work. I teach violin to help students move
through those barriers with skill, humility, and courage.

## What music has taught me

- Beauty rewards discipline, but discipline should serve joy.
- Ensemble work requires listening before leading.
- Confidence grows when hard things are made understandable.
- Local arts make a community more humane, generous, and awake.
