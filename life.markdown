I grew up in College Station, and I still value the rootedness that comes from knowing a place over time. But my curiosity and engagement never stopped there. I am interested in how the world around experiences things, and how I respond with attention, service, and imagination.

Faith and family give that habit a center. They remind me that a well-lived life is not made only of achievements, titles, or public wins. It is also made of small acts of faithfulness, repair after failure, hospitality, shared meals, music in the house, and remaining the kind of person others can trust and count on. We have found a home for our Catholic faith in [the Focolare](https://www.focolare.us/).

That is the story I want this website to tell at a glance: a man of God, a family man, a musician, an artist and an IT professional living one, seamlessly connected life.
