I grew up in College Station, and I still value the rootedness that comes from
knowing a place over time. But the deeper habit I learned was not merely to ask
what is in College Station. It was to ask how the world around me experiences
things, and how I can respond with attention, service, and imagination.

Faith and family give that habit a center. They remind me that a well-lived life
is not made only of achievements, titles, or public wins. It is also made of
small obediences, repair after failure, hospitality, shared meals, music in the
house, and a willingness to keep becoming the kind of person others can trust.

That is the story I want this website to tell at a glance: a man of God, a
family man, a musician, an artist, and an IT professional trying to live a
coherent life.
