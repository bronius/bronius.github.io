I grew up in College Station, and I still value the rootedness that comes from
knowing a place over time. But I have never wanted my curiosity to stop there.
I am usually more interested in how the world around me experiences things, and
how I can respond with attention, service, and imagination.

Faith and family give that habit a center. They remind me that a well-lived life
is not made only of achievements, titles, or public wins. It is also made of
small acts of faithfulness, repair after failure, hospitality, shared meals,
music in the house, and becoming the kind of person others can trust.

That is the story I want this website to tell at a glance: a man of God, a
family man, a musician, an artist, and an IT professional trying to live one
life instead of several disconnected ones.
